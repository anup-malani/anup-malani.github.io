A multi-week, hands-on curriculum for getting productive with [Claude Code](https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/claude-code) — the terminal-based coding agent from Anthropic. Each session is a single guide that walks through one real workflow, not a feature tour.

The full curriculum lives on GitHub at [anup-malani/ai-training](https://github.com/anup-malani/ai-training) and is licensed [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://github.com/anup-malani/ai-training/blob/main/LICENSE) — free to share and adapt for non-commercial use, with attribution.

**Start here:** read the [personal-laptop posture](https://anupmalani.com/ai-training/v2/POLICY-personal-laptop.html) (one screen — sets expectations about what Claude Code can and can't see on your machine), then open Week 1 and work through the sessions in order.

## Sessions

1. [Get Claude on your laptop, plan for the office](https://anupmalani.com/ai-training/v2/week-1/guide.html)
2. [Search your inbox like a database](https://anupmalani.com/ai-training/v2/week-2/guide.html)
3. [Draft a memo with multiple outline + draft revisions](https://anupmalani.com/ai-training/v2/week-3/guide.html)
4. [Make a plot from a spreadsheet — and revise it](https://anupmalani.com/ai-training/v2/week-4/guide.html)
5. [Make Claude remember (across sessions and across breakages)](https://anupmalani.com/ai-training/v2/week-5/guide.html)
6. [Track a corpus you can't read alone](https://anupmalani.com/ai-training/v2/week-6/guide.html)
7. [Make Claude sound like you, and not lie to you](https://anupmalani.com/ai-training/v2/week-7/guide.html)
8. [A daily brief that wakes up before you do](https://anupmalani.com/ai-training/v2/week-8/guide.html)
9. [Turn your work into a PowerPoint deck](https://anupmalani.com/ai-training/v2/week-9/guide.html)
10. [Plan a trip and coordinate everyone](https://anupmalani.com/ai-training/v2/week-10/guide.html)

**Bonus:** [Publish a personal website](https://anupmalani.com/ai-training/v2/bonus-website/guide.html)

## Feedback

Each session ends with a short Google Form linked from inside the guide. That's the channel.
